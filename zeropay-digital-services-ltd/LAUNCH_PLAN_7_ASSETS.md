### ⚠️ Critical Note: Product Hunt Risk Assessment & Multi-Product Rules
* **Existing Product Context:** The Product Hunt account has already launched **ZeroPay Money**.
* **Risk & Spam Penalty:** Product Hunt's automated moderation algorithms penalize accounts that launch multiple products in rapid succession. This risks hiding posts from the main feed, flagging the maker account for self-promotion, or triggering community downvotes.
* **Mitigation Strategy:** Do **NOT** launch all 7 products at once on Product Hunt. Stagger each Product Hunt launch by **at least 14 to 21 days** (2–3 weeks) per product.
