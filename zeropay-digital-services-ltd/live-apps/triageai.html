The problem: Millions of people die annually from cancers that are highly treatable when caught early, yet roughly half the world's population lacks access to basic diagnostic services (WHO).

In low-connectivity and low-income regions, patients often have no accessible way to determine whether a symptom warrants urgent care, and existing blood test results are frequently filed without being reviewed for early risk markers due to clinician time pressure. TriageAI addresses both the front-door triage gap and the underused-data gap.
