🌾 AgriLink — Real-time farmer-to-buyer and cold-chain logistics marketplace.

The problem: 1.3 billion tonnes of food — roughly one third of everything the world produces — is lost or wasted every year, while 733 million people go hungry (FAO/UNEP data).

Smallholder farmers in emerging markets routinely lose 30-40% of harvested crops before reaching a buyer, due to the absence of real-time market information, unreliable transport coordination, and multiple unnecessary broker layers that both delay sale and depress farmer income. AgriLink removes the information and logistics gap directly.

Market: Global agri-supply-chain software market projected to exceed $28B by 2030. Target beachhead: 2.5 million smallholder farmers across Nigeria's northern grain belt.

Grant fit: Strong fit for agri-tech and food-security grant lines: IFAD Agri-Tech innovation funds, GSMA AgriTech Innovation Fund, EU Horizon Europe Food Systems calls, and national agriculture ministry digitization grants.

The ask: $150,000 seed grant to fund 12-month pilot across 3 Nigerian states (500 farmers, 50 buyers, 20 logistics partners).
