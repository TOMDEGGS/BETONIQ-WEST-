## Field 8: Problem Statement (What problem are you solving?)
```text
1.4 billion unbanked globally / 38 million in Nigeria.

Modern digital payment architectures depend fundamentally on continuous 3G/4G/5G cellular connectivity, cloud servers, and reliable electrical grids. In emerging markets such as Nigeria—and across underserved regions worldwide—frequent telecom downtime, network congestion, power outages, and lack of rural infrastructure cause over 40% of point-of-sale (POS) digital transactions to fail or suffer severe latency. Traditional payment service providers (PSPs) and consumer neobanks operate strictly online; when the network drops, commerce stops completely, stranding micro-merchants, informal trade networks, and consumers.

Crucially, network vulnerability is not solely an emerging-market problem. High-density urban centers experience severe cell tower congestion during public events; subterranean transport networks, remote industrial/mining sites, disaster recovery zones, and developed markets suffering power grid or fiber optic failures face identical commercial paralysis when offline. Existing attempts at offline payments rely on insecure SMS/USSD channels or basic store-and-forward mechanisms that leave merchants highly vulnerable to double-spending, delayed clearing, and balance spoofing. Without hardware-grade cryptographic assurances, merchants cannot safely accept offline payments.

ZerôPâŷ Money addresses this core structural bottleneck. Instead of building another consumer neobank or merchant storefront, ZerôPâŷ operates as a resilient B2B payment infrastructure protocol beneath existing settlement rails. By decoupling payment authorization from real-time cellular internet connection, we eliminate transaction drop-off, guarantee offline security against double-spending, and restore commercial continuity across both underserved emerging markets and connectivity-compromised environments globally.
```

## Field 9: Solution & Technology Stack (How does your product work?)
```text
1.4 billion unbanked globally / 38 million in Nigeria require payment infrastructure that functions seamlessly without internet connectivity. ZerôPâŷ Money is an offline-first B2B payment infrastructure protocol operating directly below Payment Service Providers (PSPs) and settlement networks. It is explicitly NOT a consumer neobank or standard merchant application, but an underlying cryptographic protocol layer that allows existing banks, PSPs, and merchants to execute instant, secure digital transactions off-grid.

Our core technological architecture comprises five integrated pillars:

1. PUF/ECC P-256 Secure Element Terminals: Point-of-sale hardware embedded with Physical Unclonable Functions (PUF) and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC P-256) secure element microchips. This hardware root-of-trust guarantees anti-tamper key generation, device identity verification, and local offline transaction signing.
2. Offline Cryptographic Tokens (72-Hour TTL): Time-bound, cryptographically signed offline value tokens issued with a strict 72-hour Time-To-Live (TTL). These tokens validate local balances and enforce non-repudiation and anti-double-spend rules without needing an active database connection.
3. Autonomous Solar Mesh Hub Relays: Low-power, solar-charged mesh hubs that create localized, peer-to-peer wireless relay networks across off-grid commercial clusters, rural markets, and disaster zones to relay transaction proofs across terminals.
4. Paystack API Integration: Seamless backend integration with Paystack's API infrastructure (formalized through a signed Paystack merchant agreement on July 14, 2026) for batched online reconciliation, automated ledger clearing, and settlement.
5. Regulated Banking & Remittance Partners: Deep operational integrations with a licensed Microfinance Bank (MFB) partner for compliant custodial deposit holding and account provisioning, alongside an International Money Transfer Operator (IMTO) partner for cross-border corridor clearing.

When network connectivity is unavailable, payment authorization signs locally between secure element terminals using ECC P-256 cryptographic tokens. The moment any terminal or solar mesh node reconnects to a network, transactions automatically batch, reconcile, and clear through Paystack and MFB rails with zero double-spending risk.
```
