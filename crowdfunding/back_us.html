The first payment terminal that completes real transactions with zero internet, zero signal, zero excuses.

1.4 billion people worldwide have no reliable access to banking. 38 million of them are in Nigeria alone. Most of them live and trade in places where the network drops out exactly when they need it most.
The Problem
One dependency locks out hundreds of millions

Every existing digital payment system — card, mobile money, bank transfer — depends on one thing: a live internet connection at the exact moment of the transaction. In large parts of Africa, rural Europe, disaster zones, and anywhere infrastructure is unreliable, that single dependency locks hundreds of millions of people and small merchants out of the digital economy entirely.

The Solution
Signs offline. Settles the moment it can.

ZerôPâŷ Money is a payment protocol and terminal that completes and cryptographically signs a transaction entirely offline, then syncs and settles automatically the moment any connection — even a brief one — becomes available.

  1. A silicon-level Physical Unclonable Function (PUF) gives every terminal a unique, un-copyable hardware identity.
  2. ECC P-256 elliptic curve cryptography signs each transaction on-device, offline.
  3. Distance-bounding checks stop relay/replay attacks — a common weak point in offline systems.
  4. The signed transaction is held as a cryptographic offline token for up to 72 hours.
  5. The moment the terminal reaches a Solar Mesh Hub or any connectivity, it syncs and settlement completes through our licensed payment rail into the merchant's bank.

This isn't a concept. The core protocol has been formally verified using TLA+ (the same formal-verification method used to catch real bugs in systems like AWS and Azure before they ship), and our Paystack merchant integration is live today.
