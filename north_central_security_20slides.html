Regional Context

The North-Central Security Question

Benue, Kogi, Kwara, Nasarawa, Niger, Plateau, and the FCT form the bridge between north and south — our agricultural belts, forests, highways, energy infrastructure. Increasingly, they contain terrorism, banditry, kidnapping, farmer-herder conflict and rural criminality.

The enemy does not respect state boundaries. Why should our response?

New Security Architecture · Part 2

Regional Security Intelligence & Fusion Centre

Answers five questions continuously: where is the threat, who is responsible, what is their capability, what will they do next, what resource responds fastest.

UK · JTAC
US · NCTC
India · MAC (post-2008 Mumbai)
Fragmented agencies, however capable, lose to threats that move faster than their information. North-Central needs its own fusion centre.

New Security Architecture · Part 3

Community Early Warning & Early Response Network

Farmers, hunters, teachers, traditional rulers, transporters, market women see things no satellite or patrol ever will. A disciplined, legally-governed network — not a vigilante system.

Kenya · Nyumba Kumi
Philippines · Barangay Peacekeeping
Formal, well-governed community structures outperform informal vigilantism on accuracy, accountability and sustainability.

New Security Architecture · Part 5

Security Corridors, Not Administrative Boundaries

Kwara–Niger–Kainji · Niger–Kaduna–FCT · Benue–Nasarawa · Benue–Taraba · Plateau–Bauchi · Kogi–Nasarawa–Benue — the actual routes threats move today.

Governors agreed on joint corridor operations in 2025. The next step isn't another agreement — it's institutionalisation.
⏱️
New Security Architecture · Part 6

Rapid Response: The 30–60 Minute Window

Police rapid response units, military QRF, mobile teams, medical evacuation, aviation support, community liaison — all pre-positioned, not reactive.

Israel's civil defence doctrine proves what a well-rehearsed short window achieves, versus a slow chain of phone calls.

Evaluation Roadmap

5-Year Metrics & Evaluation Roadmap

YEARS 1–5I · Foundation

Fusion Centre live in all 7 states/FCT; kidnapping down 30%; Early-Warning Network in 50% of high-risk LGAs; response <90 min

YEARS 6–10II · Consolidation

Kidnapping down 60%; 100% LGAs fused; all 6 corridors institutionalised; displaced return >70%; audit compliance >90%

YEARS 11–15III · Maturity

Kidnapping down 85%; response <45 min region-wide; rising prosecutions; trust index >75%; farms fully reactivated

YEARS 16–20IV · Sustained Peace

Kidnapping industry <5% of baseline; region exports best practice; full corridor integration; self-sustaining LG financing

A twenty-year plan without five-year checkpoints is just a hope. These are the checkpoints — judge us against them publicly.

The North-Central Security Compact

Seven Commitments

1. One Threat Picture — we share intelligence
2. One Early-Warning System — we listen to communities
3. One Rapid-Response Philosophy — before massacres, not after
4. One Professional Standard — discipline, law, dignity
5. One Regional Approach — no state-border cover
6. One Development Agenda — secure and rebuild together
7. One Measure of Success — the safety and dignity of the citizen, not the volume of activity we report
Ask the summit to adopt all seven, together, as one compact.
