How It Works

Four steps.
Zero internet.

From tap to settlement — the entire transaction lifecycle is cryptographically secured and offline-first by design.

01
Tap to Pay
Customer taps NFC card or feature phone on the ZerôPâŷ Money PUF terminal. No internet. No SIM required at point of sale.
02
Cryptographic Sign
PUF hardware generates a unique ECC P-256 signed token. Distance Bounding Protocol prevents relay attacks in real time.
03
Queue & Relay
Transaction queues locally. Solar Mesh Hub relays the signed packet across the mesh network — hop by hop — until connectivity is found.
04
Batch Settle
When connectivity returns, ZerôPâŷ Money batch-settles directly via Paystack API. Licensed MFB holds float. IMTO handles remittance corridors.
