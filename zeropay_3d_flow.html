Step 1 of 6
Customer Initiates Payment (FX)
  • Sender opens the ZerôPâŷ Money app or taps their NFC card/wearable at a merchant terminal
  • Amount entered in local currency; FX rate locked at point of initiation
  • No internet connection or cellular signal required at any point
Step 2 of 6
PUF/ECC Terminal Signs an Offline Token
  • Physically Unclonable Function (PUF) generates a hardware-unique cryptographic fingerprint
  • ECC P-256 elliptic curve signing creates a tamper-proof offline transaction token
  • Dynamic challenge generation prevents token cloning or replay
Step 3 of 6
Offline Token Transmits via NFC
  • Signed token transmits over Near-Field Communication (NFC) — no cellular data used
  • Compatible with burner phones, feature phones, and low-cost wearables
  • Distance-bounding protocol blocks relay/man-in-the-middle attacks
Step 4 of 6
Solar Mesh Relay Hub Carries the Token Forward
  • Solar-powered hub with supercapacitor + lithium battery backup runs 24/7 off-grid
  • Mesh network passes the token hub-to-hub toward the nearest connected point
  • 72-hour offline token validity matches the hub's power backup window
Step 5 of 6
Recipient Receives via Burner Phone or Wearable
  • Recipient taps their device against the local terminal or hub
  • Funds appear as a pending, cryptographically-verified balance instantly
  • No smartphone or data plan required to receive money
Step 6 of 6
Reconnection Triggers Settlement
  • Once any device in the chain reconnects, tokens sync automatically to the cloud
  • Settlement routes through Paystack API to a licensed Microfinance Bank
  • Cross-border transfers complete via IMTO partner integration
The Complete Journey
End-to-End Flow: Send to Receive
  • 1. Send (FX) → 2. PUF/ECC Sign → 3. NFC Transmit
  • 4. Solar Mesh Hub → 5. NFC Receive → 6. Sync & Settle
  • Total elapsed time, fully offline: typically under 5 seconds. Settlement completes automatically upon reconnection.
