## 1. Excellence — Problem

1.4 billion people remain unbanked globally, 38 million of them in Nigeria alone. But the deeper problem is not access to a bank account — it is dependence on continuous internet connectivity for every single digital transaction. Card terminals, mobile money apps, and QR payments all fail the instant a network drops, and network drops are not rare: they are routine in rural Africa, disaster zones, maritime and aviation environments, and even in developed markets during outages, congestion, or infrastructure attacks. Every payment system on the market today, including the systems built specifically for "financial inclusion," inherits this single point of failure. The result is billions of dollars in lost transaction volume annually and merchants who cannot serve customers at the exact moment they need to.

## 2. Excellence — Solution / Innovation

ZerôPâŷ Money is an offline-first payment protocol layer, not a neobank or standard merchant acquirer. It sits below the settlement rail: a PUF (Physical Unclonable Function)-secured terminal signs transactions locally using ECC P-256 cryptography, generates a cryptographic offline token with a 72-hour time-to-live, and relays it via a solar-powered mesh hub back to a licensed settlement partner the moment any connectivity — even intermittent — becomes available. Five layers of security (silicon-level PUF, ECC P-256 signing, distance-bounding anti-relay protection, offline cryptographic tokens, and mesh relay redundancy) prevent double-spend without requiring a live connection at the point of sale.

Critically, the double-spend prevention logic has been formally verified using TLA+ model checking across 437 reachable states with zero violations found — this is a mathematical proof of correctness, not a marketing claim, and it is the kind of rigor deep-tech reviewers can independently verify.
