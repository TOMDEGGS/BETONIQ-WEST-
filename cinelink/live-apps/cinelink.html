🎬 CineLink — Turning drama and film production into a modern business enterprise.

The problem: The global film and television production market is valued at over $170 billion a year, and Nollywood alone generates an estimated $6.4 billion annually, making it Nigeria's second-largest employer after agriculture — yet the overwhelming majority of productions, especially outside the Hollywood studio system, are self-funded or funded one relationship at a time, with no structured way to reach the financiers, tax-credit schemes, or co-production partners that actually exist.

📊 Bankability ScoreAn evidence-based 0-100 score blending script readiness, team track record, budget realism, and distribution potential into one transparent number — the same logic as a credit score, applied to a film or drama project.
🤝 Funding Match EngineEvery score comes with a named list of real financing routes that fit: tax-credit financing, pre-sale advances, gap financing, equity co-production, streaming pre-sale/originals deals, brand sponsorship, and crowdfunding.
🏢 Enterprise ToolsPipeline dashboard, verified "Bankability Certified" badge, co-production/NDA/term-sheet templates, and warm introductions — the operating layer that turns a project into an investable business.

Every result is shown as a full match report: the sub-scores that make up the Bankability Score, the reasoning behind each one, and a named shortlist of real funding sources relevant to the project's market — not a black-box number.

Business model: Free forever for individual creators (2 active listings, full scoring and matching). Studio tier from $49/month for production companies and financiers managing a real pipeline. A 4% success fee applies only when a deal actually closes through the platform — CineLink only earns when creators get funded.

Market: Global film & TV financing gap is estimated in the tens of billions annually, disproportionately affecting emerging markets. Target beachhead: Nollywood and the wider West African screen industry, expanding to the global independent film and stage-drama market.

Grant fit: Strong fit for creative-economy and cultural financing programs: UNESCO International Fund for Cultural Diversity, Ford Foundation JustFilms, Doha Film Institute grants, British Council Creative Economy programs, AFDB/Africa Creative Market initiatives, and national film commission co-funding schemes.
