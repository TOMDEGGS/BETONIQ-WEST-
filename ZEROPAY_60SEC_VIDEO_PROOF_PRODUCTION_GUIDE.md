- Deliberately raw, single-take, slightly shaky is BETTER than polished — deep-tech investors are suspicious of over-produced marketing video.

- If you want a voiceover, keep it plain and factual, British-accent narration matches your existing preference for other demo assets — but text overlays alone are fine and arguably stronger here.
- Do NOT narrate or claim "zero power," "kinetic," or "ambient RF harvesting" unless that is literally what's powering the specific device on camera — the claim that survives scrutiny is zero-connectivity, and it's more than strong enough on its own.
