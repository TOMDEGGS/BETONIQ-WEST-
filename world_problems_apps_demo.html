AgriLink

1.3 billion tonnes of food — a third of everything the world produces — is lost or wasted every year, while 733 million people go hungry.

AgriLink connects smallholder farmers directly to buyers and available cold-chain trucks in real time, closing the gap between harvest and market before crops spoil.

Farmer Listing

Post crop type, quantity, harvest date and asking price in under a minute, even on a basic Android phone.

Buyer Matching

Buyers and cold-chain truckers see live listings nearby, ranked by distance and freshness window.

In-App Deal Lock

Price and pickup confirmed digitally — no more brokers taking a cut on both ends.

CivicTrack

Corruption drains over $3.6 trillion from the global economy every year — much of it extracted through delay and bribery in basic public services.

CivicTrack gives every citizen a trackable reference number for permit, ID and license applications, with real-time status updates — closing the invisible gap where informal "facilitation fees" happen.

Citizen Submission

Apply for a service, get a tracked reference number instantly — no office visit required to check status.

Agency Dashboard

Staff update status and add notes from one screen, creating a visible, timestamped audit trail.

Public Accountability

Average processing time per service type is published, making delay visible and measurable.

TriageAI

Every year, millions die from cancers that are highly treatable when caught early — but half the world's population lacks basic diagnostic access.

TriageAI offers free SMS/USSD symptom triage and clinic routing for anyone without smartphone access, plus a premium feature that reads existing blood test results and flags concerning markers for specialist follow-up.

Symptom Intake

Works over basic SMS or USSD — no data connection or smartphone needed.

Clinic Router

Matches the patient to the nearest appropriate clinic with contact details and directions.

Blood Panel Risk-Flag (Premium)

Enter existing CBC, PSA, CEA, CA-125 or AFP values — get a clear risk-flag summary, always paired with a "see a specialist" disclaimer, never a diagnosis.

Offline-First Lessons

Adaptive Difficulty

Auto-Sync Progress

SafeGuard

Romance and investment scams cost victims over 10 billion dollars a year globally, and an estimated 27.6 million people are trapped in forced labor and trafficking worldwide.

SafeGuard has two modules under one roof: ScamShield detects and reports fraud/romance scam patterns, and SafeLink gives trafficking victims an anonymous, safe path to report and reach help. Built for victim protection and prevention — not vigilante enforcement.

ScamShield: Pattern Detection

AI scans chat/email text for known scam tactics (urgency, fake investment returns, request-for-gift-cards patterns) and warns the user in real time.

ScamShield: Reverse Lookup

Cross-references submitted photos/profiles against a crowdsourced reported-scammer database before a victim sends money.

SafeLink: Anonymous Reporting

A safe, anonymous channel for trafficking tips and victim self-reporting, routed directly to NAPTIP, IOM, and the National Human Trafficking Hotline.

SafeLink: Resource Locator

Finds the nearest verified shelter, legal aid, or support NGO based on location, even under time pressure.
