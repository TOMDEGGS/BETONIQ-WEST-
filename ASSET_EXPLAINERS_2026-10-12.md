## C. TriageAI: doctors' explainer and review requirements

Files:
Review deck (7 pages, about 10 minutes): triageai-review/TriageAI_Clinical_Review_Deck.pdf
Headache question flow (8 pages): triageai-review/TriageAI_Headache_Flow_DRAFT.pdf
Editable versions: same names ending .pptx

What it is: a rule-based screening aid. Every rule can be read and checked. Not a diagnosis, not a replacement for a clinician, not a black-box AI.

How it works today, exactly as built:
1. Symptom triage matches 12 exact phrases. URGENT (within the hour): chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, stroke symptoms, severe abdominal pain, high fever with rash. MODERATE (24 to 48 hours): persistent cough, high fever, vomiting, severe headache, unexplained weight loss. Everything else: ROUTINE.
2. Blood screening compares WBC, PSA, CEA, CA-125 and AFP to fixed ranges and flags values outside them.

Known limits (stated in the deck):
1. Exact phrases only. "My chest is tight" would be rated Routine.
2. No age, sex, pregnancy, duration or severity questions.
3. No children, pregnancy or chronic conditions.
4. Fixed ranges ignore age, sex and lab method. Tumour markers can rise for harmless reasons.
5. No real clinic directory, no local language or pidgin yet.

What we ask of each reviewing doctor (about 10 minutes):
1. Is anything on pages 3 and 4 unsafe, wrong or misleading?
2. Which important symptoms or red flags are missing?
3. Is the wording clear for ordinary patients?
4. Would you consider it safe as a first-step aid, with changes, or not at all?
5. What is the single most important thing to fix first?
Reply by voice note, message or a marked-up copy.

Review rules:
1. Two medical doctors must sign off before the tool is published publicly.
2. Slides are generic. No doctor is named on them.
3. Each doctor is contacted separately, never in a joint message.
4. Each version is for one country and one language, reviewed by clinicians who practise there.
5. Nothing is sent to a doctor until Tom approves the exact message.
6. No personal health data is collected.
