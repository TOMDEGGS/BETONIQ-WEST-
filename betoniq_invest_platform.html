How BETONIQ INVEST Works

Our AI engine scores every project across 47 parameters before it ever reaches your screen.

🔍
1. Project Submission
Developers submit projects with full documentation — planning permits, feasibility studies, compliance certificates, and financial projections.
🤖
2. AI Scoring (47 Parameters)
Our AI engine evaluates macro data, project financials, location demand, regulatory status, and investor fit. Minimum score of 75/100 required for listing.
✅
3. Compliance Verification
Every project undergoes KYC/AML checks, planning approval verification, regulatory compliance review. Minimum compliance score 80/100.
💰
4. Investor Match
Our system matches projects to investors by risk appetite, investment horizon, preferred region, and minimum capital. Personalized portfolio recommendations.
📋
5. Due Diligence Package
Full feasibility study, NPV/IRR calculations, market demand analysis, and compliance certificate provided before any investment commitment.
📈
6. Quarterly Reporting
Digital quarterly performance reports, construction updates, and financial statements delivered to all investors for full portfolio transparency.
