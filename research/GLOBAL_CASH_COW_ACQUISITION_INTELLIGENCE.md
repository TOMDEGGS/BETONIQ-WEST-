## Part 2 — Financing Reality Check for a Small, Capital-Constrained Buyer

**Core paradox:** acquiring a $10M-$500M+ operating asset typically requires a cash equity check of $2M-$100M+ even under aggressive 80/20 debt structuring. A company at BETONIQ WEST's current scale (~$500-2,000/month turnover) faces a 166x-8,333x cash shortfall on the equity ticket alone, before legal/technical due diligence fees ($500k-$5M+).

Key myth-buster: debt secured against the *target's* cash flow (LBO, reserve-based lending) still requires lenders to vet the *buyer's* operational track record and sponsor equity — asset-backed does not mean buyer-blind.

### Feasibility ratings (0-10, honest assessment for a buyer our size)

1. Leveraged Buyout (LBO) — 0/10. Needs 20-40% cash equity ($2M-$200M) plus proven sector operating history. Not accessible as lead sponsor.
2. Resource/commodity-backed finance (offtake prepayments, streaming, reserve-based lending) — 1/10. Traders (Trafigura, Vitol, Glencore, Gunvor) and streamers (Franco-Nevada, Wheaton) require a Tier-1 technical operator with an HSE track record; only covers 30-50% of deal value regardless.
3. Sovereign wealth fund / strategic co-investor partnership — 3/10, the most realistic route, but ONLY as a deal originator: securing an exclusive LOI/MOU on a distressed asset, or holding indigenous licensing rights a foreign SWF legally needs a local partner for, in exchange for 2-10% free-carried equity plus a services contract (e.g. ZerôPâŷ Money payments/software integration for the acquired asset).
4. Private equity buy-in / club deal (MBI) — 1.5/10 as a fintech shell; PE sponsors fund veteran operating executives (15-20+ years running similar assets), not startups. Only path in: recruit a retired/active industry C-suite team and act as promoter/originator alongside them.
5. DFI co-financing (IFC, US DFC, Afreximbank, AFC, FMO, DEG, Proparco) — 0.5/10 direct. DFIs cap participation at 30-50% of deal cost and require 6-18 month ESG/KYC review; they fund brownfield acquisitions only when paired with real rehabilitation/expansion capex or indigenization mandates, never pure ownership transfers.

### The only theoretically real path for an entity our size
Act as deal originator, not buyer: lock exclusive rights to a distressed/divesting asset via local relationships, recruit a credible veteran operating team, then bring in an SWF/PE consortium to actually fund the deal — in exchange for a 3-10% carried equity stake plus a long-term tech/services contract. Realistic timeline: 12-18 months to assemble team and credibility; not a near-term lever. Should be treated as a long-shot background track, not something to divert current effort toward.
